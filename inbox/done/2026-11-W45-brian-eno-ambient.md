## Канон-кандидати (для неділі)
- Artist: Brian Eno (GB, 1948, Woodbridge, Suffolk; person). Roxy Music 1971–73; Obscure Records (1975–78).
- Artist: Harold Budd (US, 1936–2020; person), pianist, «soft pedal».
- Releases: Another Green World (1975), Discreet Music (1975), Ambient 1: Music for Airports (1978),
  Ambient 2: The Plateaux of Mirror (1980, з Budd), Apollo: Atmospheres and Soundtracks (1983, з Daniel Lanois, Roger Eno),
  The Pearl (1984, Budd & Eno), FOREVERANDEVERNOMORE (2022), Secret Life (2023, з Fred again..).
- Relations: Eno → coined → Ambient (лайнер-ноти Music for Airports, 1978: «as ignorable as it is interesting»);
  Eno → mentored → Fred again.. (likely); Eno → produced → Talking Heads, Bowie (Berlin trilogy), U2.
- Стилі Discogs: Ambient, Experimental, Art Rock (Another Green World), Modern Classical? (The Pearl).
